Our story

A little beauty
for the in-between.

Celisse was born from a quiet passion and the hands of a woman who believes in the beauty of soft moments, natural elegance, and heartfelt craftsmanship.

Every piece is lovingly handmade — from a grape cluster soap shaped one bead at a time to a flower candle that unfolds like a real blossom.
